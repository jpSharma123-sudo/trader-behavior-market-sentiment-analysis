# 📈 Trader Behavior vs Market Sentiment Analysis

## 📖 Project Overview

This project explores the relationship between cryptocurrency market sentiment and trader behavior, with a primary focus on Bitcoin. Using market sentiment indicators such as the Fear & Greed Index, the analysis investigates how investor emotions influence trading decisions, market movements, and overall performance.

The project combines exploratory data analysis, clustering techniques, and predictive modeling to uncover patterns that can support data-driven trading and investment strategies.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Financial markets are heavily influenced by investor psychology. Understanding how sentiment affects trading behavior can help investors and analysts:

- Identify market trends early.
- Understand investor decision-making patterns.
- Detect periods of excessive fear or greed.
- Improve trading and investment strategies.
- Support risk management decisions.
